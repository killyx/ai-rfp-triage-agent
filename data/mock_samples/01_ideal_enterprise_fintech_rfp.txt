2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROJECT OBJECTIVE
NovaFin Capital processes over 1,800 commercial credit and KYB (Know Your Business) onboarding packages per month. Currently, our compliance analysts manually review 80-to-150 page PDF packages (audited financial statements, corporate registry filings, and debt schedules), which takes an average of 9 hours per application.

We are seeking an experienced AI & Cloud Engineering partner to architect and deliver an Autonomous Document Intelligence & Credit Triage Platform with a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) compliance approval workbench.

3. CORE FUNCTIONAL SCOPE
- High-throughput PDF ingestion pipeline supporting complex financial tables and multi-column corporate filings (PyMuPDF / pdfplumber).
- LLM Extraction Engine utilizing OpenAI Structured Outputs (JSON Schema) to extract 45+ standardized credit risk indicators, UBO entities, and financial ratios.
- Semantic RAG Knowledge Base (PostgreSQL with pgvector or ChromaDB) indexing our historical credit policy manuals and past underwriting decisions.
- Real-time Confidence Scoring & Routing: applications scoring above threshold route directly to Slack Block Kit & our React Underwriter Workbench for 1-click sign-off before syncing to HubSpot CRM.
- Full SOC2-ready audit logging and observability.

4. REQUIRED TECHNOLOGY STACK
- Backend: Python 3.11+, FastAPI, Pydantic v2, PostgreSQL, pgvector, Redis
- Orchestration & AI: OpenAI API (gpt-4o / gpt-4o-mini cascading), n8n workflow automation
- Frontend Workbench: React, TypeScript, TailwindCSS
- Cloud Infrastructure: AWS (ECS Fargate, RDS), Docker, Terraform
